Pace Flow Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: December 27, 2025

1. Introduction

Welcome to Pace Flow ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy. Pace Flow is a 100% offline application — we have no servers, no backend, and no internet connectivity whatsoever. All your data exists only on your device and never leaves it.

2. Data Usage

Pace Flow does not collect, transmit, or store any of your data on external servers. All information stays on your device. Here's how we use data locally:

Approximate Location (Optional)

  • Purpose: Detect your local currency when adding transactions
  • How it works: Used once in real-time, then immediately discarded
  • Storage: NEVER stored, NEVER uploaded — we have no servers
  • Network: No internet connection is made — location is processed 100% locally
  • Control: You can enable or disable this in Settings anytime

Transaction Records

  • Purpose: Store your expense and income records
  • Storage: Stored 100% locally on your device using Apple's secure frameworks
  • Network: NEVER uploaded anywhere — we have no servers to upload to
  • Control: Delete the app to permanently remove all data

3. Information We Do NOT Collect

We want to be clear about what we do not collect:

  • No account registration or login required
  • No email addresses or personal identifiers
  • No credit card numbers or payment details
  • No usage analytics or tracking
  • No data shared with third parties
  • No cloud sync or server uploads
  • No internet connection required to use the app

4. In-App Purchases

Pace Flow offers optional in-app purchases (subscriptions and lifetime access). All payment processing is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not have access to your payment information, credit card details, or billing address. For information about how Apple handles your payment data, please refer to Apple's Privacy Policy.

5. Local Storage & Security

All your data is stored locally on your device using Apple's secure data storage frameworks (SwiftData). Your data is protected by your device's built-in security features, including Face ID, Touch ID, or passcode protection.

Because Pace Flow has no servers and makes no network connections, there is zero risk of data breaches from our side — we simply have no way to access your data.

6. Data Deletion

Since all data is stored locally on your device:

  • To delete all data: Simply delete the Pace Flow app from your device
  • To delete specific records: Use the app's built-in delete function

We do not retain any copies of your data, so once deleted from your device, it is permanently gone.

7. Children's Privacy

Pace Flow does not collect any personal information from any users, including children. Since the app works entirely offline with no data collection, it is safe for users of all ages.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected with an updated "Last updated" date at the top of this policy. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or our privacy practices, please contact us at: scooomaker@outlook.com

 
 

Thank you for trusting Pace Flow with your financial management.
